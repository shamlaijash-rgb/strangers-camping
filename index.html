The idea

Come as a stranger,
leave as family.

Strangers Camping is a two-day camp built for one simple thing: real connection, without the small talk. We pitch tents, light a fire and let the evening do the rest.

You'll share a fire with people from different cities, different jobs and different stories. There's live acoustic music, a mentalism show that will make the whole circle go quiet, BBQ smoke in the air and a night sky with zero city lights.

No experience needed, no group required. Bring yourself, a hoodie and an appetite — we'll handle the rest of the weekend.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Where exactly is the campsite?

The exact campsite location, the meeting point and the map pin are shared with confirmed campers on WhatsApp a few days before the camp. It keeps the site clean, private and safe for everyone attending.

What's included in the pass?

Tent and bedding, dinner, breakfast and all-night chai, bonfire and BBQ, both live performances, the guided trek, camp games, an on-site coordinator through the night and first-aid support. Travel to the base point isn't included.

I'm coming alone — will that be awkward?

Most of our campers come solo — it's kind of the whole point of Strangers Camping. The welcome circle, camp games and shared fire-pit seating are designed so nobody stays a stranger past the first hour.

Do I need camping experience or gear?

None at all. We set up the camp, hand you a tent and show you how to pitch it. If you'd rather bring your own tent, that's welcome too — just mention it in the booking form.

What should I pack?

A warm hoodie or jacket (nights get cold), comfortable walking shoes, a torch, your own water bottle, personal toiletries, a power bank and any medication you need. A sleeping bag is optional — bedding is provided. Leave valuables at home.

Is it safe? Are women campers okay?

Yes. The camp is supervised by coordinators all night, the site is private, and there are dedicated sleeping areas with women-only tent options available on request. Tell us in the booking form if you'd like that arrangement.

What if it rains or the camp is cancelled?

We watch the weather right up to the day. If the camp has to be moved or cancelled for safety reasons, we'll inform you on WhatsApp and your booking will be shifted to the next camp date or refunded as per the confirmation terms.

How do I pay and hold my spot?

Message us on WhatsApp with the booking form and we'll share the pass price, the payment method and a confirmation. Spots are held on a first-confirmed basis, and tent numbers are limited for this date.
